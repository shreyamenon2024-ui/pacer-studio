Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Each pair is made to order, so expect about two weeks from the day you order.

Can I return a custom pair?

Yes. You have 30 days to return any pair, custom or not, for free.

How do I find my size?

Open the size guide in the customizer. If you are between sizes, we suggest going up one.

Can I change my order after placing it?

You can change colors or size within 24 hours. Message us and we will update it.

What are the shoes made of?

A recycled knit upper, a rubber outsole, and cotton laces.
